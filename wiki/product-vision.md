# Product vision

Create an AI-native florist experience where customers express needs naturally
and the interface evolves as understanding grows.

## Business goals (BG-001 … BG-007)

1. AI-enhanced customer ordering
2. 24/7 AI-powered support
3. Personalized recommendations
4. Automate customer service activities
5. Improve inventory decision-making
6. Optimize fulfillment and delivery
7. Enhance engagement and retention

## Principles (summary)

Thought before form · Knowledge before navigation · Shared understanding before
recommendation · Experiences earn attention · Continuity before immediacy ·
Latest relevant intent wins · **AI interprets; domain services validate**
