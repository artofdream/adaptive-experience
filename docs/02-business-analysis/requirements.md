# Requirements Specification

## Functional Requirements
- FR-001 Accept free-form customer intent.
- FR-002 Generate context-aware thought-completion suggestions.
- FR-003 Display Shared Understanding.
- FR-004 Allow correction of inferred information.
- FR-005 Seed discovery using seasonality, inventory and business context.
- FR-006 Generate recommendations from customer intent.
- FR-007 Display product details, price and delivery eligibility.
- FR-008 Allow product selection.
- FR-009 Collect recipient information.
- FR-010 Collect delivery date and window.
- FR-011 Validate delivery availability.
- FR-012 Collect gift message.
- FR-013 Provide order review.
- FR-014 Confirm order placement.
- FR-015 Provide AI support during discovery.
- FR-016 Provide AI support during fulfillment.
- FR-017 Show delivery status.
- FR-018 Retrieve prior orders.
- FR-019 Support reorder.
- FR-020 Allow modification before reorder.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- NFR-001 Maintain visual stability during updates.
- NFR-002 Refresh only affected experience regions.
- NFR-003 Acknowledge actions immediately.
- NFR-004 Support asynchronous processing.
- NFR-005 Prevent stale results from overwriting newer intent.
- NFR-006 Support keyboard navigation.
- NFR-007 Meet accessibility requirements.
- NFR-008 Protect customer and order data.
- NFR-009 Support modular growth.
- NFR-010 Log key decisions and actions.
