## Overlay experiences

## Functional flow
1. Customer arrives.
2. Discovery is seeded with contextual prompts.
3. Customer enters partial intent.
4. Thought-completion suggestions appear.
5. Shared Understanding updates.
6. Recommendations emerge.
7. Customer selects a product.
8. Delivery planning appears.
9. Support remains available.
10. Customer confirms the order.
11. Workspace evolves into tracking.
