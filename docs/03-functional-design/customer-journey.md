# MVP Customer Journey

> Maps the seven customer stages to the active tiles and canonical scope.

| Step | Stage | Active tile(s) | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Enter / Discovery | T-01 Conversation and Intent | MVP |
| 2 | Describe need / Shared Understanding | T-01, T-02 Intent Summary | MVP |
| 3 | Review recommendations | T-03 Curated Recommendations | MVP |
| 4 | Customize | T-04 Product Selection and Customization | MVP |
| 5 | Confirm delivery | T-05 Delivery and Recipient, T-06 Order Summary | MVP |
| 6 | Review, pay, and order | T-06 Order Summary, T-07 Checkout and Confirmation | MVP |
| 7 | Track delivery | T-08 Order Tracking | MVP |

The **Automated Support Overlay (ASO)** for FR-009 is available across every
MVP stage; it is a cross-cutting overlay, not a journey step (see
`automated-support-overlay.md` and ADR-004).

Support Escalation (T-09 / FR-006) and reorder / customer memory are Future
capabilities outside the MVP journey.
