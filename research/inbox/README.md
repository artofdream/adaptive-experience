# Inbox

Fleeting notes captured from Obsidian (or pasted from Claude) before triage.

Use [`../templates/inbox-note.md`](../templates/inbox-note.md). Tag ready items
`#promote`. After promotion, retag `#promoted` or delete the note.

After a meaningful agent or CI run, also draft a **lesson candidate** with
[`../templates/lesson-candidate.md`](../templates/lesson-candidate.md) and
[`../after-run-lesson-candidate.md`](../after-run-lesson-candidate.md). Those
notes stay candidates until `@aea-knowledge-guardian` promotes them. Do not
self-promote.

This folder may stay nearly empty; that is normal.
